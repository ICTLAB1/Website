# Photographs of the people who run the company

Files here are served at `/team/<name>`. Only one thing points at them today:
the `directorPhoto` setting, edited under **Settings → Who runs the business**,
which the about page shows beside the director's name.

## What is here

| File | Who | Supplied |
|---|---|---|
| `abhinav-jain.webp` | Abhinav Jain, Managing Director | By the business, September 2026 |

Supplied as a 1023 × 1537 PNG of 1.7 MB, resized to 640 pixels wide and
converted to WebP, which is 26 KB — twice the width it renders at, and small
enough not to be the largest thing on the page. Nothing was cropped or
retouched: the framing, including the wordmark on the wall behind, is the
photograph as it was taken.

## The rule this directory exists to enforce

**A photograph goes up only with the consent of the person in it, and comes down
when they leave or withdraw it.**

This is the one directory in `public/` holding pictures of identifiable people,
which makes it the one place where the question is not "is this artwork licensed"
but "does this person still want their face on a public website". Those are
different questions with different answers, and the second one can change without
anybody sending a takedown notice.

So: no photograph of a person who has not agreed to it, no photograph left up
after they have left the company, and no stock photography of strangers
presented as this company's people. If a director changes, replace the file
*and* the name and designation together in Settings — the site pairs them from
one place precisely so a new name cannot end up under the previous person's
face.
