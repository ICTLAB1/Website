# Gap report: ThoughtSol (thoughtsol.in) vs TechZoid

Read on 27 November 2026 from ThoughtSol's public pages (home page and 30 inner pages, text and stylesheet
only) and our live sitemap (549 URLs). This compares **topics and page types**. It does not copy their
wording, images, logos, client names, numbers or awards, none of which are ours to use.

## 3. Gaps: topics they cover and we do not

"Fit" is whether it matches what the catalogue already lets us say. **Confirm** means it needs a fact only the business
can give (that TechZoid delivers the service). We should not publish a service page for something we do not offer.

| # | Gap | Evidence on our site | Fit | Suggested action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Data centre and hybrid IT hub** | No page; we sell HPE, Dell and Cisco servers, storage and networking | High: the products exist | Hub page grouping our server, storage and networking brands, with links to the existing brand hubs and quote forms |
| 2 | **ZTNA, WAF, DLP, email security** | `/firewall-security`, `/antivirus-endpoint-security` and `/services/cybersecurity` only | High: Fortinet, Sophos, SonicWall and Cisco products are in the catalogue | One page per solution, built from catalogue products, quote-only |

## 4. Suggested order

1. **Pages we can build now from our own catalogue (1, 2, 3):** data-centre hub, the four security solution pages and Microsoft AI licensing guides. No new claims needed.
2. **Questions for the business (4 to 9, 11):** which services does TechZoid actually deliver? Answers decide which pages exist.
3. **Proof (10, 12):** the largest credibility gap. Needs client logos with permission, real testimonials and any genuine awards.

## 5. Design notes (inspiration only)

- Their site is a stock Bootstrap 5 template: default blue `#0d6efd`, Helvetica Neue / Arial, slick and swiper carousels,
  Font Awesome icons. It is a generic look.
- Layout ideas that work and are fair to reuse in our own style: a five-card service grid, a partner strip that leads
  into sub-pages, a short counter row (only with true numbers), a "how we work" five-step strip on the AV page.
- Nothing from their stylesheet, imagery or copy should be reused.
